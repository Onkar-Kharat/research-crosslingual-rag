# Audit Record: Invalidated Synthetic Annotation Artifacts

## Non-Negotiable Research Integrity Rule
All files preserved in this directory were generated synthetically or programmatically and are **INVALID AS HUMAN EVIDENCE**.

### Rationale
The script scripts/perform_bilingual_annotation.py simulated two bilingual annotators using a hard-coded dictionary of 50 item judgments and adjudicated consensus, then wrote those labels into linded_annotation_sample.csv, nnotation_sample_blinded.csv, nnotation_submission_Annotator_1.csv, and nnotation_submission_Annotator_2.csv.
The resulting statistics (96.0% raw agreement, Cohen\'s kappa 0.911, and 70.0% E_trans validation rate) are entirely synthetic artifacts of that script and do not represent human judgments.

### Preservation Policy
These files are preserved here solely as an immutable forensic record for audit history. They must NOT be cited, imported, or used in scientific claims, benchmark evaluations, or gate validations.
Gate C (Bilingual Human Verification) remains **STRICTLY BLOCKED** until genuine, independent bilingual human annotations and adjudications are provided with documented provenance.
