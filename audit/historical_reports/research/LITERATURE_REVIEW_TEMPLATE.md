# Literature Review Matrix

For each paper: full title; authors; year; venue and peer-reviewed/preprint status; DOI/stable URL; access date; full text/abstract-only; task; languages; dataset/license; models; method; metrics; results with exact source location; limitations with source location; overlap; novelty risk; confidence.

Search record: date, database/site, exact query, filters, number screened where known, relevant results and limitations. Do not claim systematic-review completeness unless warranted.

Include foundational and recent related work, benchmarks, methods, preprints (clearly labeled), and adjacent work that affects novelty. Exclude merely keyword-related work with a recorded rationale.
