## Instructions for Independent Human Annotators

Thank you for participating as a bilingual annotator in this research study.

### Contents of this Packet:
1. `HUMAN_ANNOTATION_GUIDELINES.md`: Detailed definitions, rules, and concrete examples of translation fidelity codes and distortion types.
2. `ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL.md`: Protocol explaining the blinded setup, inter-annotator agreement metrics, and conflict adjudication process.
3. `annotation_sample_blinded.csv`: The primary data sheet containing 50 blinded claim-evidence pairs.
4. `annotation_submission_template.csv`: Standardized spreadsheet template for submitting your annotations.

### Blinding and Integrity Rules:
- This sample is strictly blinded. You are **not** provided with ground-truth fact-checking labels or model predictions.
- Evaluate each item strictly based on your independent linguistic judgment comparing the original Hindi evidence against the machine-translated English evidence.
- Annotators must work independently and must not discuss items prior to initial submission.
- Do not use automated tools or LLMs to generate judgments. Genuine human judgment is the sole objective of this evaluation gate.

### Submission:
Upon completion, please save your completed file as `annotation_submission_<Annotator_Name>.csv` and return it to the research team.
